About TheoryLabs

TheoryLabs is an aggregate Teamfight Tactics analytics and theorycrafting tool for studying champions, items, teammates, traits and underexplored composition ideas. It turns many historical ranked matches into evidence you can read before or after you play, so you can understand why a line works instead of copying a tier list.

How a player uses it

  1. Pick a question. “Which carries are doing well while almost nobody plays them?” or “I want to carry with this champion. What does the evidence say?”
  2. Read the evidence. Every number comes with its sample size and the balance window (patch) it was measured in. Small samples are flagged and pulled toward the average.
  3. Decide for yourself. TheoryLabs explains patterns; it never tells you what to do in a game.

Discover

The Discover page lists carry lines that perform well relative to how rarely they are played, ranked by an Opportunity Score that rewards strong, low-usage lines and discounts thin evidence. Opening a line shows how often committed boards actually hit three stars, which teammates, item packages and traits appear with it, and how those boards placed.

Champion Investigation

Champion Investigation answers “I want this champion to be my carry: what can I realistically build around it, and why?” It summarises common completed items and item pairs, the item components those items are built from, frequent teammates and recurring unit cores, trait counts, and the star-level evidence, each with how many boards support it.

Experiments

Experiments is a theorycrafting notebook: composition ideas worth testing, with research notes. Every entry carries an evidence stamp. An idea stays Theorycrafted until real match evidence is attached; it is never presented as a proven or new composition. Entries are written by the site operator; the page is read-only.

Observed evidence vs theorycrafting

Observed
A statistic computed from indexed matches of the active data source.
Theorycrafted
An idea or reasoning that has not been measured. Shown separately and labelled.

The note at the top of each data page names the active data source. When the site runs on demo data, every number is synthetic and is labelled that way; it is never presented as real match evidence.

What TheoryLabs is not

TheoryLabs is not a live gameplay assistant. It does not read or react to a game in progress, has no overlay, does not track or scout opponents, does not give real-time or moment-to-moment instructions, and does not automate any in-game decision or skill. All evidence is aggregate and historical, for study outside the game.
