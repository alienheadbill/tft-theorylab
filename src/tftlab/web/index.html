WHY THIS IS DIFFERENT

We count the misses.

Evidence in this dashboard is currently always OBSERVED — a real statistical result from indexed matches. Two more tiers are reserved for later: VARIANT for a related line the system hasn't independently measured yet, and THEORYCRAFTED for a board the system actually synthesizes rather than observes. Nothing on this page is theorycrafted today.

01

Commitment first

A carry enters the sample when it holds at least two completed items, even if the reroll never reaches 3★.

02

Hit vs. miss

We separate games that actually hit 3★ from committed games that miss, exposing both ceiling and failure risk.

03

Sample shrinkage

Small samples are pulled toward baseline performance so a handful of lucky games cannot outrank a reliable line.

04

Rarity matters

The Opportunity Score rewards strong low-usage lines instead of simply reproducing the most-played meta boards.
