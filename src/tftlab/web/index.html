How to read this notebook

  1. Commitment first

    A carry enters the sample when it holds at least two completed items, even if the reroll never reaches 3★. The misses are counted, not quietly dropped.

  2. Hit vs. miss

    Games that actually hit 3★ are kept apart from committed games that missed, so you can see both the ceiling and what happens when it goes wrong.

  3. Sample shrinkage

    Small samples are pulled toward baseline performance, so a handful of lucky games can't outrank a line with real evidence behind it.

  4. Rarity matters

    The Opportunity Score rewards strong, low-usage lines instead of re-ranking the boards everyone already plays.
